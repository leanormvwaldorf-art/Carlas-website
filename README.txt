CARLA'S LITTLE WEBSITE — Acode edition

FILES
- index.html: website structure
- style.css: colours, layout and responsive design
- script.js: buttons, pop-up, mood generator, note reveal and mobile menu
- assets/carla.jpg: the portrait used on the home page

OPEN IN ACODE
1. Extract carla-website-acode.zip.
2. In Acode, open the extracted folder (not just index.html).
3. Open index.html and tap Preview / the play button.
4. Keep the assets folder beside index.html. Do not move carla.jpg out of it.

CUSTOMISE
- Change text in index.html.
- Change colours near the top of style.css in the :root section.
- Change mood results and reminder messages in script.js.
- Replace assets/carla.jpg with another photo, keeping the exact filename.

The Google Fonts are optional; the page uses fallback fonts if you are offline.
